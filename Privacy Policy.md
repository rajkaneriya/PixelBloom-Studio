# Privacy Policy

Welcome to **PixelBloom Studio**. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile games and applications. This privacy policy is applicable to all games of **PixelBloom Studio**.

## Personal Information

PixelBloom Studio does **not** knowingly collect or store any personally identifiable information from users.

If you believe that we have inadvertently collected personal information, please contact us, and we will promptly remove it.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, share, or distribute any information or documents about individual users with any third party, except to comply with applicable laws or valid legal processes or to protect the personal safety of our users or the public.

## Advertisements

Our games may display advertisements provided by trusted third-party ad networks. These Third Parties ad networks are carefully selected to ensure compliance with applicable privacy laws, and we confirm that they are compliant with COPPA, this Privacy Policy and the rules, policies and guidelines of the applicable Platform Provider in performance of services for PixelBloom Studio. Note that if the User of the Kids Apps clicks on any of these ads, the User will leave our Kids Apps and go to the advertisers’ site which the User selected, and the advertisers may use cookies and other tracking technologies to collect Non-personal Information and/or Kids personally identifiable information.

## Children's Privacy

We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 in violation of COPPA, and if in the event that a user identifies himself or herself as a child under the age of 13 through a support request, we will not collect, store or use, and will delete in a secure manner, any personal information of such user.

## Analytics

Our apps may use trusted third-party analytics services, including **Firebase Analytics**, to help us improve our games.

These services may collect non-personal information such as:

- Device type and operating system
- Country and language settings
- App usage and gameplay statistics
- Crash reports and performance data

This information is used only to improve the quality, stability, and user experience of our apps.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information processed through our services. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

PixelBloom Studio reserves the right to change, alter, or update this Privacy Statement at any time. As a result, we encourage you to review this privacy statement periodically.
