About PixelBloom

We are dreamers, designers, and developers.

Our Story

PixelBloom Games started with a simple idea, We never planned to build a brand, we simply wanted to create free, high-quality learning & puzzle apps for everyone. No paywalls. Just kindness, creativity, and love. What started as a small act of giving has now grown into something much bigger, a global movement reaching over in 192 countries.

We don't just build games, we build immersive worlds where players can explore, create, and connect. Our team is passionate about pushing the boundaries of mobile gaming, ensuring that every title we release is a testament to our commitment to quality and innovation.

Every game we release With zero tolerance for bugs, the outstanding mobile games we build are a result of our sheer dedication and passion that we put into our work.

Our Mission

"To create a safe, engaging and innovative digital playground where imagination knows no bounds. We strive to develop games that not only entertain but also inspire creativity and bring a smile to every player's face."
